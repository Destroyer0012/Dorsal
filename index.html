DCML High-Yield MBBS Notes

1. 3-Neuron Sensory Chain Overview

  • 1st Order Neuron: Pseudounipolar cell in DRG. Central axon enters posterior column without synapsing in spinal cord.
  • 2nd Order Neuron: Nucleus Gracilis/Cuneatus in Caudal Medulla. Axons form Internal Arcuate Fibers $\to$ Decussate $\to$ Medial Lemniscus.
  • 3rd Order Neuron: VPL Nucleus of Thalamus $\to$ Projects via PLIC to Somatosensory Cortex S1 (Brodmann 3, 1, 2).

2. Somatotopic Mapping Rule

Fasciculus Gracilis (Medial): Carries fibers from $T_6$ and below (Lower body/legs). Projects to ACA territory.
Fasciculus Cuneatus (Lateral): Carries fibers from $C_2-T_5$ (Upper body/arms). Projects to MCA territory.

3. High-Yield Clinical Pearls

  • Tabes Dorsalis: Demyelination of posterior columns in Neurosyphilis. Leads to sensory ataxia, positive Romberg sign, and loss of 128Hz vibration.
  • Brown-Séquard Syndrome: Spinal hemisection. Causes ipsilateral loss of DCML modalities below the lesion level (since decussation happens higher in the medulla!).
  • Medial Medullary Syndrome: ASA occlusion causing Medial Lemniscus ischemia $\to$ contralateral loss of vibration & proprioception.
